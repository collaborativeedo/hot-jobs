# Hot Jobs backlog

What's planned next, and what's been proposed but not yet decided. Newest decisions first.

## In scope for the next build

- **Drop listings whose link no longer works.** Each night, check every listing's link before publishing. A listing whose page returns "not found" or "gone" (404/410), or now redirects to a generic careers or home page, is dropped. Sites that refuse automated visitors (401/403/429) are left alone, not treated as dead. Record the count dropped each night on the staff dashboard. *(Approved by Matt, Oct 29, 2026.)*

## Proposed, not yet decided

- **"Was this job still open?" prompt.** When someone clicks through to a listing and comes back to the widget within about a minute, show one small line on that listing only: "Was this job still open? Yes / No." Nothing shows otherwise. Answers, and quick returns in general, go to the staff dashboard as a dead-link and quality signal.
- **One "Report a problem" link in the widget footer**, not on each listing, for wrong pay, wrong employer, or suspicious postings.
- **Weekly quality spot-check.** 25 random listings, about 30 minutes, without slowing registry growth.
- **Public "How Hot Jobs works" page**: no ads, no tracking cookies, no data sold; where listings and pay averages come from; a contact for employer corrections and opt-outs.
- **Case-manager shortlists.** Pick a few listings and get a short link, QR code, or printable one-page sheet for a client.
- **Filters for common barriers**: on an LTD bus line, no degree required, training provided, fair-chance employers; a Spanish-language interface.
- **Employer pages** showing each employer's views and apply clicks, with a way to correct their name or careers page.
- **Training and funding links on listings** (LCC programs, on-the-job training dollars).
- **Dashboard measures**: days open per posting; postings added and removed by employer each week (expansion and layoff-aversion signals); share of postings with stated pay by sector.
