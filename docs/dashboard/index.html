Lane County Hot Jobs: Staff Dashboard

For workforce and economic development staff. Updated after each nightly refresh. Widget traffic

Trends:

Today

Coverage over time

How much of Lane County's hiring the nightly search reaches. "Employers with openings" counts distinct employers that had at least one open posting found that night. "Employer hiring systems searched" counts the careers sites and hiring systems the run reads directly.

Openings today by sector and place

Openings shown to job seekers today (postings up to 14 days old). The change column compares with the same day one week earlier.

How long postings stay open

A posting counts as closed once the nightly run has missed it 3 nights in a row; days open runs from the night it was first seen to the night it was marked closed. Dates are when the nightly run first saw a posting, and the run only began Oct 6, 2026, so early figures understate how long postings stay open (anything already posted before then looks newer than it is).

Longest-open postings now

The 20 open postings first seen longest ago. Long-open postings can mean hard-to-fill roles, or standing "always hiring" posts.

Postings added and removed by employer

Added: postings first seen in the last 7 days. Removed: postings marked closed in the last 7 days. The flagged employers below are signals worth a call, not conclusions: postings close for many reasons, including positions being filled, and a burst of new postings can be a re-post. Employers that are not named on their postings are left out.

Possible expansion

Open postings up by 3 or more since a week ago, and by at least half (or from none).

Watch list

Open postings down by 3 or more since a week ago, and by at least half (or to none).

Most change this week

Employers with the most postings added plus removed in the last 7 days. "Open wk ago" is what each had open 7 days ago; "Net" is the change in open postings since then.

Pay stated by employers

Share of postings shown today where the employer states the pay. The typical pay for an occupation that the widget shows when a posting gives none does not count here.

Most openings today

Employers named on the most postings shown today.

New to the registry this week

Employers first seen in the last 7 days, most openings first.

Hiring systems searched directly

Each employer careers site or hiring system the nightly run reads, with last night's Lane County openings and result.

Nightly processes

The automated jobs behind Hot Jobs: when each last ran, how long it took, and whether it finished cleanly. A failed or missing nightly run means the widget is showing the previous day's listings.

Automated jobs

Last 10 runs of each. "Problems" counts runs that ended in failure or timed out; cancelled runs are left out. Times are Pacific.

Last nightly refresh, step by step

Minutes spent on each part of the most recent nightly run.

Warnings from recent nightly runs

Notes the run logged about sources that failed, refused automated visits or were skipped. Most are routine; the same warning night after night is worth a look.

Data: Oregon Employment Department QualityInfo Job Finder and WorkSource Oregon listings, plus employers' own hiring systems, collected nightly by Collaborative Economic Development Oregon for Lane Workforce Partnership. Sector ("type of work") comes from each posting's federal occupation code where available, otherwise from the job title. Counts are of postings, not positions; one posting can cover several hires, and not every opening is posted online. Large jumps on a single day usually reflect a change in what the run reads (a new source or better employer naming), not a change in hiring. This page is not linked from the job seeker widget.
