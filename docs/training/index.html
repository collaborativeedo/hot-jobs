Lane County Hot Jobs · Counselor guide

Help job seekers find real openings, fast

Four one-minute videos, hands-on practice in the live tool, and a quick check. About 10 minutes, at your own pace.

1Search and filters

Start broad, then narrow. Every filter shows how many openings it would add.

  • Search matches job titles, skills and employers: "CDL," "bilingual," "PeaceHealth." Spacing and punctuation don't matter.
  • Town, type of work, kind of employer and hours combine. The count at the top always shows how many openings match.
  • Clear all filters starts over. Remote jobs are a separate "Where you'd work" choice and never mixed into Lane County counts.

2Fewer barriers

Four filters for the things that most often stand between a client and a job.

  • Near an LTD bus stop: the workplace is about a quarter mile or less from a Lane Transit District stop.
  • No degree needed: the posting doesn't ask for a college degree. Training provided: paid or on-the-job training, or an apprenticeship.
  • Fair-chance employer: the employer has a confirmed fair-chance policy, or the posting says a record won't automatically rule someone out. Unlike the other filters, these narrow together: a job must match every one you pick.

3Reading a listing

Each line tells you something you'd otherwise have to click through to learn.

  • "Pay:" is the employer's own posted pay. "Pay not listed" shows the Lane County average for that kind of job, clearly marked as an average.
  • "Link checked" means Hot Jobs opened the posting and it was still up. "Posted by the employer" means the link goes to the employer's own hiring site.
  • View and apply opens the original posting; the note beside it says where the application goes. Listings show how long ago they were posted, and Hot Jobs refreshes every night.
Try itFind a listing that shows the employer's own pay, and one that shows the local average. What's the difference in how they're labeled? Go to the live tool ↓

4Share a list, report a problem

Hand a client their own short list, and help keep the listings accurate.

  • Make a list for someone: pick up to 12 jobs, then copy a link, show a QR code, or print a one-page list. The link always shows the latest status of each job.
  • Let us know (at the bottom of the list) reports a closed job, a broken link or wrong details. Nothing personal is collected.
  • Missing an employer you know is hiring? Send its careers page to the Hot Jobs team and it's added to the nightly search.
